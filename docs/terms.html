2. What WindoM provides

WindoM replaces the Chrome new tab page with a customizable productivity dashboard. It provides:

  • A clock, weather display, and daily quote
  • To-do list and quick-link dock
  • Customizable background images (Unsplash or your own uploads)
  • Focus timer with animated overlay
  • Tab history sidebar and search
  • Optional account features: settings sync across devices, Google Calendar integration, Gmail inbox preview, and Spotify now-playing display
  • Finance widget with stock and cryptocurrency price tracking
